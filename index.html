The game

Your city. Your score.

Kit has $40, a tent under a bridge nobody finished, and very fast hands. Everything in Ember Bay is open: the shops, the cars, the apartments on the hill.

🕵️

Steal smart

Pickpocket, break into houses at night, crack safes and pull jewellery heists. Witnesses remember you.

🚗

Drive anything

Fifteen cars with their own engines, real damage, a cockpit view and a radio. Own them, tune them, call them over.

👥

Play with friends

Host a room, share a 6-letter code and up to five of you share the same city. Ride in each other's cars.

🏙️

A living island

Day and night, rain and storms, traffic, cops, shops with real people behind the counter and lights in every window.

🏠

Build a life

Buy apartments you can walk into, a garage for your cars, a wardrobe for your look, and a bed to sleep off the heat.

💾

Your progress, kept

Separate saves for solo and online, stored on your own PC. Runs offline; the internet is only for playing together.

Play with friends

Same city, same night

No servers to set up, no ports to open. Everyone just needs the same version, and the game keeps itself up to date.

Install Ember Bay

Everyone downloads it from this page. It updates itself when there's a new version.

One of you hosts

Play with friends → Host. You get a 6-letter room code. Copy it to your group chat.

Everyone else joins

Play with friends → Join, type the code, and you appear next to the host.

Find each other

Friends show as green arrows on the map. Press F next to a friend's car to ride with them, T to chat.
